Manifesto · a work in progress

Many brains off, connected as a network

The human brain has always been offline. No one has ever spoken to it directly: everything passes through a filter that decides what is said, to whom and when. Offnets does not invent a new idea. It copies the architecture that trust has always had.

A method, not a brain

Offnets is the name of the method and the protocol that turn spoken life (conversations, meetings, talks) into organized knowledge: lessons, patterns, decisions, always with the source and with who taught it. No brain rules the others. Each person has their own, and what is shared is the method.

Three kinds of brain

A person's. A company's, made of the brains that make it. A community's. A person can link to companies and communities, and what belongs to one never crosses into another.

Each owner decides

Each brain lives with its owner, outside the internet, and nothing leaves unless the owner releases it, item by item. The exchange can be knowledge, purpose, a cause or money. It is never the sale of what someone knows: what is licensed is the tool, never the contents of a brain.

A network, not a warehouse

There is no central cloud holding every brain. Each one stays at home, and they talk by protocol, with receipts. What we do today person to person, tomorrow each person's AI agents will do among themselves, each with its owner's brain, and only with what the owner released.

By invitation only

No one joins alone. Whoever is already in invites, signs the entry and answers for it. The network grows from trust to trust, the way real knowledge grows.

The knowledge that matters cannot be scraped. It can only be given, and only to those who deserve trust.

Off-portal in 2002. Off-net in 2026.

In 2001, Yavox was born on a sailboat and signed itself moving networks: the idea that the network moves with people, and that every mobile phone is an antenna. In 2002, we took services outside the carriers' portals (what was called off-portal), each creator under their own brand, and nothing reached anyone without opt-in. By 2007 that network reached 21 million people in Brazil.

Twenty-four years later, the move is the same, one layer up. AI took the whole internet. The intelligence that matters is still outside it, in people's heads. Offnets are the networks that stay outside: one for each person who knows something, connected to each other, with opt-in again, item by item.
