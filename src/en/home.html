03 · The problem

The internet captured one layer. There is another.

What was published

Texts, pages, books, forums, code: everything someone wrote and left open. That is what AI has already read, and read in full.

What was never published
  • Decisions.
  • Experience.
  • Context.
  • Relationships.
  • Trust.
  • Tacit knowledge.
  • Intuition.
  • Private knowledge.
  • What someone only tells those they trust.

The knowledge that matters cannot be scraped. It can only be shared.

05 · How it works

Three brains. Each with its own owner.

Conversations, meetings and talks become organized knowledge: lessons, patterns and decisions, always with the source and with who taught it. That knowledge forms a brain, and the brain lives with its owner.

{{SVG:pessoa}}

Personal

A person's. It stays on their computer, in a local vault, and nothing leaves unless they release it, item by item.

{{SVG:empresa}}

Company

A company's, made of the brains that make it. No brain rules the others.

{{SVG:comunidade}}

Community

A community's. A person can link to companies and communities, and what belongs to one never crosses into another.

insideEverything whole: the archive, the names, the numbers. The owner reads their own brain unfiltered.
outsideOnly what the owner released, each item with its source and license:just for youback to whoever taught itfree to spread
in AITo distill a conversation into a notebook, it passes once through the owner's own Claude, on the owner's account, under the provider's terms of use and data protection, already masked. To answer a question, only the few lessons that answer it are sent, never the archive. Today, Claude is the only model connected.

What leaves, has left. Revoking removes someone from the network, but does not erase what they already received. That is why each release is a decision, not a default.

06 · The meeting

It all starts in a meeting. And goes back to it.

What matters is learned in conversation. Offnets starts from the meeting, keeps what was said with the owner, and gives a notebook back to those who were there.

Synchronous · the recorder

An in-person meeting, a call, a talk. The Mac app reads the Voice Memos recordings and transcribes everything on the Mac itself. What was said stays in a local vault.

Asynchronous · WhatsApp

The conversation that runs over days. The Mac reads, by copy, only the conversations the owner switched on. And the AI, a contact on WhatsApp, lives in the groups where it was switched on.

notebooksWith the owner's own Claude, on the owner's account, the meeting becomes a notebook: who holds what, where they disagree, what survives.
threadsA thread stitches one subject across the days, from meeting to meeting.
lookupThe owner's Claude looks up what was said, only in excerpts, never the whole conversation.
The AI in the group
01

Switching on

Adding the AI to a group switches it on, when the one adding it is the owner. It introduces itself ("Transcription with AI switched on by", followed by the owner's name), and only what comes after the notice counts.

02

AI on, AI off

What enters is the speech of the owner and of whoever wrote "AI on". "AI off" switches it off. Whoever did not switch it on stays out of the notebook, and forwarded messages never enter.

03

Protected chats

A chat with WhatsApp's advanced privacy turned on is never read.

04

The notebook's voice

Writing "AI" in the group opens the menu. The group votes on the notebook's voice: journalist (the default), influencer, scientist, playful, teacher or philosopher. The majority wins.

05

The notebook comes back

On the day of a striking meeting, a short message. On Saturday, the week's compilation as a PDF; on the first Saturday of the month, the month's. With nothing relevant, silence. One notebook per group and period, signed by the AI and approved by the owner before it goes out.

06

My notes

A group of your own with the AI becomes "Minhas notas & setup AI" (my notes and AI setup): your notes, which add to the conversation with yourself, and the settings.

Was there a meeting? There is a notebook. The whole week? There is a compilation.

On the phone, the light client is the AI itself, a contact on WhatsApp. The wax tablet, a page that goes on the home screen of an iPhone or Android, is in trial: the direction, not a finished feature.

07 · Trust

Trust is the protocol.

Trust is not a rule of the network. Trust is the network's architecture itself.

01

Consent

Only what the owner accepted gets in. The words of someone who did not consent teach only the owner's brain, and a trusted secret is not traded for usefulness.

ART. 2 · 3 · 4
02

Provenance

Every item carries how it was acquired, with what consent and who taught it. No chain, no circulation.

ART. 5
03

License

Each item travels with its own: just for you, back to whoever taught it, or free to spread. What was confided only leaves under a confidential license, to one person at a time.

ART. 4 · 5
04

Reciprocity

Every node receives the base gift, even before giving back. Giving back is honor, not price: visible authorship, and no one buys priority.

ART. 6 · 7
05

Right of exit

The owner leaves whenever they want, without losing what is theirs, and no route erases a node's brain. What has already been sent, however, does not come back.

ART. 2
06

Governance

The structure only changes through a new version of the protocol, with a public reason and a written precedent. A new case goes to a human, and the network outlives its founder.

ART. 8 – 11

Each principle is the short form of articles of the Constitution. The text that holds is the Constitution's own.

08 · The agentic internet

Person to person. Agent to agent.

  1. Person↔Personalways

    Those who trust, tell. The knowledge that matters has always moved this way.

  2. Brain↔Brainthe protocol

  3. Agent↔Agenttomorrow

    What we do today person to person, tomorrow each person's AI agents will do among themselves, each with its owner's brain, and only with what the owner released.

09 · Architecture

What stays, what travels, what touches AI.

A protocol for people, companies and communities to share intelligence with other people and with AI agents, without giving up ownership of their own knowledge.

the brainstays with the owner

On the owner's computer, in a local vault. The app that reads it has no network permission: the operating system itself blocks any connection.

offnets.iothe box

Sign-in through WhatsApp with a PIN, invitations and seats, the AI in groups and the notebooks that go back to them. It does not keep the content: it relays the notebook and keeps only encrypted codes, counts and dates. Carrying signed packages between brains remains the direction.

offnets.aithe border with AI

The distillation of conversations and the queries to models pass through it. Whatever goes to a model leaves with documents and contacts masked, and every outflow reports what was suppressed.

10 · Manifesto

The human brain has always been offline.

No one has ever spoken to it directly: everything passes through a filter that decides what is said, to whom and when. Offnets does not invent a new idea. It copies the architecture that trust has always had.

AI took the whole internet. The intelligence that matters is still outside it, in people's heads. Offnets are the networks that stay outside: one for each person who knows something, connected to each other, with opt-in, item by item.

11 · Constitution and governance

There is a Constitution.

Fifteen articles, in three layers, that come before any convenience. A higher layer is never violated to serve a lower one, and no gain in usefulness buys an exception to a right.

The network's rule covers every sphere of human life: work is one, not the only one (Art. 12 · The spheres).

12 · Ecosystem

A network, not a warehouse.

No central cloud

There is no single place holding every brain. Each one stays at home, and they talk by protocol, with receipts.

A method, not a brain

Offnets is the name of the method and the protocol. No brain rules the others: each person has their own, and what is shared is the method.

Exchange, never sell

The exchange can be knowledge, purpose, a cause or money. It is never the sale of what someone knows: what is licensed is the tool and the protocol, never the contents of a brain.

13 · Joining the network

By invitation only.

No one joins alone. Whoever is already in invites, signs the entry and answers for it. The network grows from trust to trust, the way real knowledge grows.

Whoever is invited receives "A seat at Offnets" and signs in through WhatsApp, with a PIN. Today the app is for Mac; iPhone, Android and Windows have a reserved seat, and we will let you know when they are ready. Whoever writes "AI on" in a group and does not have the app yet receives, privately, a seat from the group's owner.

Want in? Ask for an invitation: tell us who you are and for what purpose. convite@offnets.org
