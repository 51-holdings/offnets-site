Constitution of the network · version 4 · 25 October 2026 · the founder's precepts

The rules that come before any convenience

AI has already scraped the internet. Most knowledge is not there: it is in people's heads. That knowledge cannot be scraped, only given, and it only reaches those who deserve trust. That is why, in this network, justice is not a brake: it is the mechanism by which knowledge arrives. As with a blood bank, a voluntary, screened and traced donation is worth more than any forced or purchased collection.

The articles hold in layers. A higher layer is never violated to serve a lower one, and no gain in usefulness buys an exception to a right.

Precepts · before any rule
ART. 0 · THE FOUNDER'S PRECEPTS

Offnets was born so that lived knowledge is not lost and so that each person is honored for what they teach. Before any rule, these precepts hold:

  1. I · Equal dignity. Every person has the same dignity, whatever their faith, origin, color, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age or condition. The network will never be used to diminish anyone for who they are.
  2. II · The common good above gain. The network exists for human flourishing, not for the profit of whoever controls it. Gain is welcome when it serves the common good; when it betrays it, it loses its reason to exist.
  3. III · The prudence of those who have lived. The knowledge worth most is the practical wisdom of those who lived the cases. The network keeps it with the name of whoever taught it, and never puts the machine in the place of human judgment.
  4. IV · Truth. No one, neither the machine nor the founder, asserts what they do not know or hides what they ought to say. Silence is allowed; deceit, never.
  5. V · Trust is given, not taken. The knowledge that is not on the internet only reaches those who deserve trust. Whoever betrays that trust even once dries up the source for everyone.
  6. VI · The weakest first. Every decision first asks who is the most vulnerable and whether they end up better or worse off. Inequality is only justified if it benefits those who have least.
  7. VII · Power with a brake. Whoever guards the network is guarded too: no one, not even the founder, governs without a written reason, without limits and without being accountable.
  8. VIII · No to violence and hatred. The network gives no strength to those who exist to do harm to others.

No version of the protocol changes these precepts.

Signed: Andreas Blazoudakis, founder, with the collaboration of Cristiane Mendes, who brought Sandel to Offnets. In the founder's absence, she guards these precepts and answers for them.

Purpose
ART. 1 · PURPOSE

The network exists to create wisdom and honor those who teach, in every sphere of human life: work is one of them, not the only one. It does not exist to accumulate data. What it rewards is the lesson that still holds years from now.

Rights · no one is merely a means
ART. 2 · CONSENT AND EXIT

Only what the owner accepted gets in. They can leave whenever they want without losing what is already theirs, and no route erases a node's brain.

ART. 3 · THE PERSON QUOTED

The words of someone who did not consent (a group member, a meeting participant) teach only the owner's brain. If they leave it, they leave as an anonymous pattern, or not at all.

ART. 4 · THE TRUSTED SECRET

What was confided is not traded for usefulness. It only leaves under a confidential license, by human decision, to one person at a time, with no right to pass it on.

ART. 16 · WHAT THE NETWORK NEVER SERVES

The network does not serve to watch, persecute, discriminate against or exclude people, nor to compile lists of people by faith, origin, sexual orientation, gender identity, politics or health. It does not serve to incite hatred against any group, nor to pressure vulnerable people into giving money. Nor does it serve to deceive, defraud, imitate someone's voice or face, or plan violence. Whoever uses the network this way loses access to it, whoever they are.

Distribution · what circulates, to whom and under what title
ART. 5 · LINEAGE

Every item carries how it was acquired (source and consent) and through which license it passed from hand to hand. No chain, no circulation.

ART. 6 · THE GIFT FLOOR

Every node receives the base gift, even before giving anything back. Reciprocity adds; it never takes away the floor. The advantage of those who started first is only justified if it benefits those who arrive last.

ART. 7 · HONOR, NOT PRICE

Reciprocity is recognition: visible authorship, who taught what. It is never currency, no one buys priority, and the scoreboard shows contribution without ranking people. The commercial license sells the tool and the protocol, never the knowledge of a brain.

ART. 12 · THE SPHERES

Each purpose is a sphere, with its own rule of exchange: sharing, mentoring, reciprocity, gift or market. What is given in one sphere is not converted into currency, priority or advantage in another. Where the exchange is not a market, there is no price, balance or scoreboard, only recognition. Every node is born with all spheres closed: opening is the owner's decision, per person and per sphere. Faith, ethnic origin, politics and health are sensitive spheres: they open only with their own, highlighted consent. In matters of faith, the machine shows the readings and who taught them; the community decides the doctrine.

ART. 13 · THE EMISSARY SERVES THE ENDS OF WHOEVER SENDS IT

The brain decides what it is looking for and why; the emissary decides only which way to go and how to carry it. It never carries more authority than it was granted, and that authority ends when the mission ends.

ART. 14 · SILENCE IS ALLOWED; INVENTION IS NOT

Every brain may answer, answer in part, say it does not have it, or prefer not to answer, without giving a reason. No brain asserts what it does not know or inflates its own certainty.

ART. 15 · POSSESSION BELONGS TO THE OWNER; USE IS SHARED BY CONSENT

A brain stays in the custody of whoever formed it, on their own machine. The network does not keep brains: it creates the conditions for them to meet when there is a reason, and the meeting creates no permanent tie.

ART. 17 · MANY HOUSES, NONE ABOVE THE OTHERS

Each community is sovereign in its own house and its own network. No house reads, maps or seeks out the members of another. Exchange between houses only happens by invitation from both, lesson by lesson, and never carries along whoever taught without their consent. The network takes no side between creeds, traditions or nations.

Governance · how the law changes
ART. 8 · ONE STRUCTURE, A VERSIONED PROTOCOL

On the node, everything is automatic and no one improvises a way to extract, store or exchange. The structure in which brains talk only changes through a new version of the protocol.

ART. 9 · PROPOSALS WITH PUBLIC REASONS

Any node may propose a change. The proposal goes through the four-chairs test: "would I accept this if I did not know whether I would be the founder, an old node, a new node or the person quoted?". The decision publishes its reason; a decision without a written reason does not stand.

ART. 10 · CASE LAW

Every dilemma decided becomes a precedent (facts, decision, reason, article applied). A new case, or one that touches a person's rights, goes to a human. The machine optimizes only within the space the rights leave open.

ART. 11 · THE NETWORK OUTLIVES ITS FOUNDER

Continuity is secured by a technical will and by shared keys. As the network grows, its founder stops writing every rule and governs by vetoes that guard the rights.

ART. 18 · HOW HARM IS CUT OFF

The cut is proportionate and follows a process: warning, suspension of the license and disconnection. Whoever is cut off knows the facts, can defend themselves and can appeal. The decision is human, has a written reason and becomes a precedent (Art. 10). When a life is in immediate danger, the brake comes first and the process after, within 7 days.

ART. 19 · THE GUARDIAN IS GUARDED TOO

Whoever guards the network can brake and disconnect nodes, but does not read brains, does not sell anyone's knowledge and hands data to no government without a court order, which will be published whenever the law allows. Changing the Precepts or the Rights requires the founder's signature and a public reason; in his absence, that of whoever guards the precepts. Every year, whoever guards the network publishes how many nodes were cut off, under which article, and how many government requests it received.

ART. 20 · THE PROTOCOL IS NOT FOR SALE

The commercial license grants the right to use the tool and the protocol, never the right to change the Constitution, nor a vote in guarding the network, nor access to brains. No investment, partnership or acquisition transfers control of the Precepts or the Rights.

The Constitution is inspired by Michael Sandel's Justice. Arts. 13 to 15 (version 3, 8 October 2026) draw on Aristotle: Nicomachean Ethics III, IV and VIII, and Politics II. Version 4 (25 October 2026) adds the founder's precepts and Arts. 16 to 20, inspired by Aristotle (Politics I and III, Nicomachean Ethics IV, V, VI and VIII) and by Sandel (Justice, What Money Can't Buy, The Tyranny of Merit). The constitution and the protocol are our own. Like the manifesto, it is clay: it changes by version, always dated. The foundations are in Where it comes from.
